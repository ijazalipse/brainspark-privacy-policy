# BrainSpark Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to BrainSpark, a puzzle and brain-training game for mobile devices. This Privacy Policy explains how BrainSpark handles information when you use our mobile application. It covers information stored by the app, information processed by third-party advertising services, and the choices available to you.

**Developer:** Ijaz Ali  
**Privacy contact:** [ijaz.ali.se@gmail.com](mailto:ijaz.ali.se@gmail.com)

## 2. Information Stored by BrainSpark

BrainSpark may store game information locally on your device, including:

- Game progress, scores, experience points, and levels.
- Virtual coins, hints, achievements, and streaks.
- Daily challenge completion and reward progress.
- Your sound and haptics preferences.
- Whether you have completed the introductory onboarding.

This information is used to operate the game and remember your preferences. It is not necessarily transmitted to our own servers.

You can use the in-app reset progress feature, where available, to reset supported game progress. Uninstalling the app or clearing its local data may also remove locally stored information.

## 3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

BrainSpark uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Depending on the SDK version, configuration, consent choices, and features used, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process information such as:

- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
- Interactions with the app and advertisements.
- Diagnostic information relating to performance, crashes, and fraud prevention.

Google and its advertising partners may use relevant information to provide, measure, personalize, or limit advertisements, and to detect fraud and maintain their services, as permitted by applicable settings and law.

For further information, review [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, BrainSpark uses applicable consent mechanisms to present advertising privacy choices.

Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be able to choose whether certain information is used for personalized advertising or other advertising purposes. You can access available privacy options through the app's Settings screen when those options are required.

You can manage certain advertising identifier settings through your device settings. Changing these settings does not necessarily prevent all advertising or all information processing.

## 5. Data Sharing

BrainSpark's integrated advertising service may transmit information to Google and relevant advertising partners as described in this policy.

We do not claim that information processed by third-party advertising services is collected or controlled exclusively by the BrainSpark developer. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game preferences and progress stored locally are generally retained until they are reset, removed by clearing the app's data, or deleted through uninstalling the app.

Advertising-related information is handled according to the relevant third-party service's retention practices and applicable settings and legal requirements.

The in-app reset feature does not necessarily delete information held by third-party advertising providers. Requests concerning that information may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

## 7. Children's Privacy

BrainSpark is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through an account-registration system. Third-party advertising services may process device or advertising information, subject to their configuration and applicable requirements.

If you believe that information has been collected inappropriately, contact us using the details below.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the app and its data handling in a manner appropriate to its features. Information transmitted by third-party services is subject to their security practices and applicable safeguards. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when BrainSpark's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy at the same policy URL and update the revision date.
